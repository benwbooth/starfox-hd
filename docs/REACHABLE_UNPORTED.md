**68 strats are PLACED by ported maps but unregistered** → they spawn a
nullshape/proxy today and are behaviorally wrong in-game. This is the reachable
priority queue (sharper than docs/FUNCTION_LEDGER.md, which lists all labels
regardless of placement). Verify each against the ROM before porting; a few may
be map-side or alias an already-registered strat under a different name.

## Recommended batching
Port in groups by shared mechanics to amortize infra: (1) the BHOLEEXIT/BLACKHOLE
warp-arming set; (2) the tank/wall/ground family; (3) projectiles/hazards;
(4) base set-pieces; then the two capstones. Each group = one focused lane in a
new module or the bosses.rs section style, registered + map-resolved, with a
parity test, verified via the tier-1/tier-2 harnesses.
